## 🚀 What I'm building — Medinovance

**[Medinovance](https://medinovance.tech)** is an AI-powered medical knowledge ecosystem: intelligent, adaptable, and locally relevant healthcare technology, built for emerging healthcare systems with a global outlook.

- 📚 **Medinovance Academy** — medical education platform for learners and exam preparation. Live, in limited beta, 2,800+ questions.
- 🏥 **Medinovance Care** — clinical decision support platform delivering localized treatment guidelines, drug databases, and clinical topics to healthcare providers across specialties in low- and middle-income countries. In development.
- 🤖 **Medinovance AI** — intelligence layer across the ecosystem, customizable to the needs of specific countries and healthcare facilities.
